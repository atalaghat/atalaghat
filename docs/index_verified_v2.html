Research and engineering practice

I work at the intersection of pavement engineering, AI-driven digital twins and construction management. My research interests include computer vision for pavement condition assessment, predictive modelling, BIM/IoT integration and maintenance decision support.

I completed my Ph.D. in Construction Engineering and Management at Amirkabir University of Technology in January 2026. My international research experience includes collaboration on the DigiPave project and a visiting research period at the University of Oulu from October 2024 to April 2025.

My industry work includes technical office management, project planning and controls, quantity surveying, cost estimation, contract administration and delay claims. I serve as Technical and Contracts Affairs Manager at Sahar Sazeh Novin Paydar.
